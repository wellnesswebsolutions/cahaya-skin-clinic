Body & Wellness Treatments

Drainage, recovery and wellness treatments designed to support your body from the inside out.

  • Pressotherapy

    £70
    Advanced lymphatic drainage

    Our pressotherapy treatment is an advanced form of lymphatic drainage massage. The suit uses specially designed garments that gently inflate to precise, pre-set pressures — often described as having a team of massage therapists working on the body simultaneously to flush out excess fluid, toxins and metabolic waste. As relaxing as it is, it is the workout equivalent of 20 minutes of intense treadmill activity.

  • Lymphatic Drainage Massage

    £80
    60 minutes, full body

    Our full body, 60 minute manual lymphatic drainage massage is customised entirely to you. As we are trained in a number of different drainage and massage techniques, we can design this treatment around your goals. The traditional Vodder method can be used alongside the Földi method to encourage drainage and expel toxins. Wood therapy and Brazilian lymphatic massage techniques can also be used for those wanting more body contouring.

  • Wood Therapy Massage

    £90
    60 minutes, contouring

    This 60 minute treatment focuses on body contouring alongside lymphatic drainage, using a range of wooden apparatus to sculpt and define your natural contours. A great pre-event treatment.

  • Menopause Massage

    £75
    Hormonal support

    Help yourself to feel lighter, less sluggish, less bloated and more relaxed, with a massage aimed at alleviating some of the discomfort associated with hormonal changes.

  • Post-Op Massage

    Recovery focused

    This treatment focuses on healing. Using gentle drainage techniques with red light and oxygen therapy, together with our PEMF mat, you can expect to relax while you recover.

  • Body Wraps

    Total detox

    Starting on our vibration plate, we then use castor oil and/or magnesium in conjunction with our PEMF infrared sauna dome — you can expect to burn up to 300 calories within 30 minutes. This treatment helps remove toxins from the body and helps to ground and re-centre, while being totally relaxing.

  • Oxygen Therapy

    £45
    90%+ oxygen LED dome

    Elevate your wellness and deepen cellular activation using our 90%+ oxygen LED light dome. A fantastic option for avid exercisers, or anyone struggling with persistent illness, lack of energy or dull skin — and a great addition to fat loss treatments. Studies show that breathing a higher concentration of oxygen increases the amount of fat exhaled from the body.
