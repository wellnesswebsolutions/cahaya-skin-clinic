Advanced Skin Treatments

Clinically validated, results-driven treatments using medical grade devices. Advanced and express treatments can be performed as stand-alone appointments or added on to any other treatment.

  • Algae Peel

    £85
    Bio microneedling

    The revolutionary algae peel has taken the industry by storm. A form of bio microneedling, this peel has an improved ingredient delivery system, enhances skin renewal and offers improved structural support for the skin. A must for all skin types and concerns.

  • Vitamin Infusion Therapy

    £90
    Mesotherapy with NCTF 135HA

    This procedure incorporates microneedling with NCTF 135. The micro-punctures created during microneedling allow for a deeper infusion of the 24 amino acids, 12 vitamins and 6 mineral ingredients that make up the award-winning collagen bio-stimulator, NCTF 135HA.

  • Microneedling Remodelling

    £85
    With polynucleotides & exosomes

    Microneedling uses a medical device to create tiny wounds in the target area. By fabricating a minor injury, the body's healing process awakens, igniting the production of collagen and elastin — and a more youthful complexion. It can also minimise the effects of scarring and stretch marks, reduce pore size and reduce wrinkle depth. What sets our microneedling apart is the incorporation of salmon DNA and exosome therapy to accelerate results and stimulate cell-to-cell communication. Every microneedling treatment includes a complimentary skin-healing oxygen and LED light treatment.

  • Fractional Radiofrequency

    £85
    Lift and tighten

    Fractional radio frequency tightens over-stretched collagen fibres for an instant lift, and plays a huge role in helping your skin create more collagen and elastin — helping you to age backwards, naturally.

  • Radiofrequency with Microneedling

    Face £120 · Eyes £80
    The ultimate lift and tighten

    This combination couples the benefits of radio frequency with the advantages of microneedling. Radio frequency machinery sends radio waves to selectively target and heat the deep layer of your skin, stimulating the fibroblast cells to contract and produce new elastin and collagen fibres.

  • Hair Growth Restoration Therapy

    from £85
    Scalp microneedling

    Hair growth stimulators are applied to the scalp using microneedling technology. The hair-restoring ingredients penetrate individual hair follicles and initiate growth through the tiny channels made by the microneedling. A popular treatment for those with thinning hair, or anyone wanting to encourage growth and volume.
