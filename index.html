About us

Honest, judgement-free, results-driven

Skin, Slim & Laser Solutions — formerly The Cahaya Skin Clinic — is an advanced skin, laser and non-surgical body clinic in Beverley and Hessle. “Cahaya” is Indonesian for “glow” — and that is our aim.

Our founder Lucy-Anne started our journey in Australia, inspired by pioneering treatments down under, before bringing those advancements to East Yorkshire. As proud members of the British Medical Laser Association, we hold all necessary qualifications so our clients are as safe as possible. Every treatment here is clinically validated — if it does not deliver results, we simply do not offer it.

Delivered by advanced practitioners using clinically validated technology only.

4. Aftercare

Prescriptive homecare, before & after photos, and a review of your skin's response.
