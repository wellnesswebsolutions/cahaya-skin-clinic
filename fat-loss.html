Small area covers one cup (upper abdomen, lower abdomen, bingo wings, chin); large area covers two cups (inside or outside thigh, back of thigh, love handles, back fat, upper and lower abdomen).

Safety first

Am I a candidate for fat loss treatments?

The following conditions prevent treatment with all of our non-surgical liposuction treatments. If you are unsure, please raise it at consultation — some conditions need individual advice rather than an outright no.

  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Vascular diseases
  • Pacemakers
  • Thrombosis or thrombophlebitis
  • Heart conditions
  • Prescribed anti-coagulant (blood thinner) medications
  • Steroid medications
  • Low or high blood pressure medicines — seek advice
  • Transplant patients
  • Cancer patients
  • Tumours
  • Users of large metal prosthetics — seek advice
  • Diabetes
  • Epilepsy
